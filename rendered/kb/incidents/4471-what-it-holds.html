parser · the queued fix

The shared parser reads every date month first. All eleven tenants on file write the day first, so any day up to the 12th comes back with month and day swapped: 7 April becomes 4 July. Reports reach us from every tenant and every channel. This is the fix already queued.

hint · the booking-v3 form

The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at the three booking-v3 tenants (alder.physio, copper.studio, pale.harbour) type what the hint says, month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. Same symptom, different cause: the parser fix does not touch the hint. Every one of these came through the form.

one-day-move · not a month flip

Five reports describe a move of exactly one day: a Tuesday shown as the Monday, one meeting on two days in Lisbon and Toronto, an invite that arrived before the booking. The desk read them as a one-day move and footnoted them under parser by tenant. They are a time-zone or day-boundary fault and had no ticket until now.

Why the merge hid this

The desk merges on subject line alone (decision 0001). “Booking on the wrong date” from a booking-v3 form and from the app share a subject and land in one ticket, though the dedupe explorer shows they are different causes. Intake’s parser and hint tags are guesses from the reporter’s browser locale: 23 hint tags sit on parser reports and 15 parser tags on hint reports. The tenant’s locale and form decide, not the browser.

What to do

  • When a report arrives, place it by tenant and channel with causeOf, not by intake tag.
  • Re-read the incident page after the parser fix ships: its “one issue” line will be wrong until then.
